Our invoice approval process works like this: when an invoice arrives by email, an AP clerk downloads the PDF attachment and prints it. The printed invoice is placed in the manager's physical inbox. The manager reviews and signs it, sometimes within the same day but often it sits for 2-3 days if they are traveling. The signed invoice is then scanned and emailed back to AP. AP manually re-enters the invoice amount, vendor name, and GL code into the ERP system. The ERP entry is then reviewed by a second AP specialist before payment is released. If there is a discrepancy between the scanned invoice and the ERP entry, the invoice is returned to the original clerk to investigate, which can take another 2-5 business days.
